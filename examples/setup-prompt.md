# Set up LEADNOTE in one go

`/leadnote-setup` asks one question at a time. If you would rather hand over everything at once, fill in the
template below (any language is fine) and paste it after the command:

```
/leadnote-setup <paste the filled-in template here>
```

LEADNOTE then shows one short summary ("This is what I understood") and only asks what is missing or contradicts
itself. Don't have it written down? Use the prompt in part 2 to let ChatGPT or Claude write it from your website.

## 1. The template

Copy it, fill in what you know, leave out what you don't. The more concrete, the better the leads.

```
WHAT I SELL: <your product or service, in one or two lines>
PRICE: <rough price or deal size, e.g. "EUR 500 a month" or "EUR 20k projects">
IDEAL CUSTOMER: <the kind of company, what they do, for whom, how many people, where>
BEST CLIENTS: <2 or 3 of your best clients, with their websites>
WHY THEY ARE GOOD CLIENTS: <what they have in common>
BAD FITS: <1 or 2 kinds of companies that look right but are not, and why>
MUST-HAVES: <without this, never a fit>
NICE-TO-HAVES: <makes a company a better fit; put the most important first>
DEAL-BREAKERS: <one of these and it is a no: too small, too big, chains, consumers only, ...>
BUYING SIGNALS: <what on a website shows they need you now: hiring for X, a new office, software Y, news>
WHO I WANT TO SPEAK TO: <the role: owner, operations manager, head of IT, ...>
A GOOD FIRST CALL: <what counts as success, and the objections you hear most>
AREA: <country, region or cities>
```

A filled-in example:

```
WHAT I SELL: White-label backup and disaster recovery that IT companies resell to their clients.
PRICE: EUR 300 to 2,000 a month per reseller.
IDEAL CUSTOMER: Independent IT companies that manage the IT of offices, schools or care on a contract, 5-50 people.
BEST CLIENTS: <website 1>, <website 2>
WHY THEY ARE GOOD CLIENTS: fixed monthly contract per workstation, own service desk, Microsoft 365 management.
BAD FITS: big IT groups (they build it themselves); web design agencies that call themselves "IT".
MUST-HAVES: manages IT for businesses on a contract; business clients; independent.
NICE-TO-HAVES: already mentions backup; Microsoft 365 management; 10-50 people; clients in care or education.
DEAL-BREAKERS: consumer repair shops; web/marketing agencies; groups of 250+ people; freelancers.
BUYING SIGNALS: hiring a service desk engineer; new office or merger; mentions NIS2 or ISO 27001.
WHO I WANT TO SPEAK TO: the owner in firms under 20 people, else the service manager.
A GOOD FIRST CALL: a 20-minute demo booked. Objections: "we use Veeam already", "clients don't ask for it".
AREA: Utrecht and Amersfoort.
```

## 2. Let an AI write it from your website

Paste this into ChatGPT, Claude or any chat AI, with your website address filled in. Check the answer before you
use it: the AI guesses where your site says nothing, and it marks those guesses.

```
I sell to other businesses and want a precise list of companies to call. Help me describe my ideal customer.

My website: <your website>
Extra about my offer (optional): <price, best clients, what you never want>

Read my website (and anything I added above). Then fill in this template, in short, concrete lines. Describe
customers by things you can see on THEIR website or Google Maps listing (what they do, for whom, size, services,
job openings, software they name), not by feelings. Mark every line you had to guess with "(guess)". Do not invent
client names. Keep it under 300 words. Then ask me the 3 questions whose answers would make it most precise.

WHAT I SELL:
PRICE:
IDEAL CUSTOMER:
BEST CLIENTS:
WHY THEY ARE GOOD CLIENTS:
BAD FITS:
MUST-HAVES:
NICE-TO-HAVES:
DEAL-BREAKERS:
BUYING SIGNALS:
WHO I WANT TO SPEAK TO:
A GOOD FIRST CALL:
AREA:
```

Answer its 3 questions, let it update the template, and paste the result after `/leadnote-setup`.
