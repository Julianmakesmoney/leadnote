# Criteria: IT managed service providers, Utrecht

## Offer
A white-label backup and disaster-recovery service that IT companies resell to their own business clients, under
their own brand, billed per workstation per month.

## Price
About EUR 300 to 2,000 a month per reseller, depending on how many clients they put on it. A buyer needs at least
a few dozen managed workstations at their clients to make it worth it; that is an IT firm of roughly 5 people or more.

## Ideal customer
An independent IT company in the Netherlands that runs IT for other businesses on a contract (managed services,
"ontzorgen", a service desk), with 5 to 50 people, whose clients are offices, schools or healthcare.

## Lookalikes from best clients
- Both best clients sell a fixed monthly IT contract per workstation ("per werkplek per maand").
- Both have their own service desk and name Microsoft 365 management as a core service.
- 10 to 30 people, a team page with photos, founded more than 10 years ago.
- Clients named by sector: healthcare, education, accountancy.

## Must-haves
- M1: Runs IT for other businesses on an ongoing contract (managed services, service desk, monitoring, "ontzorgen").
- M2: Its clients are businesses or institutions, not consumers.

## Nice-to-haves
- N1 (weight 3): Offers or mentions backup, continuity or disaster recovery already.
- N2 (weight 2): Microsoft 365 or cloud workplace management is a named service.
- N3 (weight 2): 10 to 50 people (a team page, "our 14 colleagues").
- N4 (weight 1): Founded more than 5 years ago.
- N5 (weight 1): Clients in a named sector (healthcare, education, legal, accountancy).

## Deal-breakers
- D1: Computer or phone repair shops and computer stores for consumers.
- D2: Web design, marketing or app-development agencies without IT management.
- D3: Pure software vendors, recruiters, IT trainers, or hardware resellers without support contracts.
- D4: IT groups with more than 250 people, national chains, or firms that a large group has taken over.
- D5: Freelancers or one-person firms that only do projects.

## Buying signals
- S1 (weight 3): Hiring for the service desk, system administration or engineering (support is growing).
- S2 (weight 2): A new office, a merger or an acquisition mentioned on the site.
- S3 (weight 2): Security or compliance (NIS2, ISO 27001) named as a new service or focus.
- S4 (weight 1): News or blog posts from the last 6 months.

## Who to ask for
The owner or managing director (in firms under 20 people), else the operations or service manager. Not the sales
desk. Dutch titles: "eigenaar", "directeur", "operationeel manager", "servicemanager".

## What a good first call looks like
Success is a 20-minute online demo booked with the owner or the service manager. Open on something concrete on
their site (a service, a sector, a job opening). Expect "we already use Veeam or Acronis" and "our clients don't ask
for it": answer that resellers keep their own tool and add a margin product, and that NIS2 makes clients ask.
Tone: peer to peer, curious, short. Language: the language of the company's website.
