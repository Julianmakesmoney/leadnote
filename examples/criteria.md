# Criteria: IT managed service providers, Utrecht

## What I sell
A white-label backup and disaster-recovery service that IT companies resell to their own business clients.

## Fit: yes
- An IT company that runs IT for other businesses on a contract: managed services, IT support for offices,
  "ontzorgen", a service desk, monitoring, Microsoft 365 / cloud management, backup.
- Its clients are businesses (SMEs, offices, schools, healthcare), not consumers.
- Roughly 5 to 50 people (a team page, "our 12 colleagues", or a few job openings are good signs).
- Independent, or part of a small group.

## Fit: partly
- Right kind of company but bigger than 50 people, or clearly a one- or two-person firm that does manage clients.
- Managed services is one of several lines (e.g. mostly network cabling, telecom or software, with some support).
- The site does not say who their clients are.

## Fit: no (deal-breakers)
- Computer or phone repair shops for consumers, computer stores.
- Web design, marketing or app-development agencies without IT management.
- Pure software vendors, recruiters, IT trainers, hardware resellers without support contracts.
- National or international IT groups with more than 250 people.
- Freelancers who only do projects (no ongoing clients).

## Signals that make it stronger
- They already mention backup, continuity, security or Microsoft 365 management.
- They are hiring (service desk, system administrator, engineer).
- Clients in a named sector (healthcare, education, legal, accountancy).
- Founded more than 5 years ago.

## Opening line
Touch on something concrete on their site: a service they name, a sector they serve, a job they are hiring for.
Tone: peer to peer, curious, short. Language: the language of the company's website.

## Assumptions
none
